# Computer-Based Testing Facility (CBTF): Student Guide & Testing Instructions

Welcome to the Computer-Based Testing Facility (CBTF). The CBTF provides a secure, quiet, and standardized environment for taking computer-based quizzes and exams.

To ensure a smooth testing experience, please read these instructions carefully before scheduling and arriving for your exam.

---

## 1. Scheduling Your Quiz or Exam

You will schedule all CBTF tests through your course portal on Canvas.

### How to Schedule

1. **Access the Scheduler:** Log in to Canvas, navigate to your course, and open your course dashboard or the designated CBTF scheduling link.
2. **Find Your Assessment:** Locate the quiz or exam row and click **Schedule Exam** (or the schedule icon).
3. **Select Your Time:**
   - Choose your preferred time of day (Morning or Afternoon).
   - Select an available date within your testing window.
   - Select an available time slot.
4. **Confirm Your Booking:** Review your reservation details and confirm your reservation. You will see a confirmation screen with your scheduled date and time window.

### ⚠️ Critical Advice: Schedule Early!

> [!WARNING]
> **Do not wait until the last day of the testing window to schedule!**
> CBTF seating capacity is strictly capped per hour to prevent crowding. Slots on the final day often fill up completely well in advance. If you delay and all remaining slots fill up, you will be unable to schedule a time and may receive a zero for the assessment. Book your preferred time as soon as the scheduling window opens.

## 2. Before You Arrive: Crucial Preparation

### A. Generate Duo Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Passcodes

When you log into the CBTF testing computer and Canvas, you will be required to authenticate via university two-factor authentication (Duo).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Phones and smartwatches are strictly forbidden at testing desks.**
> You will **not** be able to tap an "Approve" push notification on your smartphone or smartwatch during login.
>
> **You must generate Duo passcodes in advance:**
>
> 1. Prior to entering the testing center, generate a batch of Duo bypass passcodes (via the university 2FA management portal, Duo Mobile app passcode generator, or hardware token).
> 2. Write down your passcodes on a small slip of paper or memorize one to use at login.
> 3. If you do not have a passcode when you sit down, you will have to step out of the lab to retrieve your device, which consumes your testing time.

### B. Restroom & Personal Needs

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Use the restroom before arriving at the CBTF.**
> Standard exam sessions last up to 60 minutes. To protect test security and maintain a quiet, distraction-free environment for all test takers, **restroom breaks are not permitted** once you check in.
>
> _In the event of an unavoidable medical emergency, the proctor must record your departure, your exam timer will continue running without pause, and no extra time will be granted._ Take care of all personal needs beforehand!

---

## 3. Arriving at the Testing Facility

### Timing

- **Check-in opens 5 minutes before your scheduled start time.**
- Aim to arrive **5 to 10 minutes early** so you can store your belongings and have your ID ready.
- If you arrive more than 5 minutes early, please wait quietly in the lobby area outside until your check-in window opens.

### What to Bring

- **Physical Student ID Card:** You **must** present your official university physical photo ID card. Photos of IDs on phones or digital IDs cannot be accepted because phones must remain in cubbies.
- **Duo Passcode / Hardware Token:** For logging in at your workstation.
- _(Optional)_ A pair of basic foam earplugs or a clear, label-free water bottle (placed on the floor beside your desk).

### What NOT to Bring to Your Desk (Storage Policy)

All personal belongings must be stowed in the designated storage cubbies before you approach the proctor desk:

- **Backpacks, tote bags, purses, and coats/jackets.**
- **All personal electronics:** Cell phones, smartphones, smartwatches, fitness trackers, tablets, wireless headphones, Bluetooth earbuds, and calculators (unless explicitly authorized by your instructor).
- **Notes, notebooks, textbooks, or personal scratch paper.**

> [!CAUTION]
> **Power down all phones and alarms before placing them into your backpack.**
> Having any unauthorized electronic device or notes on your person (at your desk, in your pocket, or on your wrist) is an automatic academic integrity violation resulting in immediate exam termination.

---

## 4. Check-In & Starting Your Exam

1. **Stow Belongings:** Place your backpack, jacket, phone, smartwatch, and other items securely into a cubby.
2. **Approach the Proctor Desk:** Have your physical Student ID in hand.
3. **Identity Verification & Card Hand-Off:**
   - The proctor will scan or swipe your Student ID and verify your photo identity.
   - **The proctor retains your physical Student ID card** at the check-in desk for the entire duration of your exam.
4. **Receive Materials:**
   - The proctor will provide you with **one sheet of official stamped scratch paper** and a sharpened pencil. You may not use your own paper or writing tools.
5. **Proceed to Your Assigned Seat:**
   - The proctor will assign you a specific workstation (e.g., _Seat #14_).
   - Walk directly to your assigned computer. Workstations are sequentially assigned to prevent cheating and maintain room spacing—**do not sit at any other desk.**

---

## 5. Taking Your Quiz or Test

1. **Log In:** Wake the monitor and log into the workstation using your university credentials and your pre-generated Duo passcode.
2. **Launch Your Exam:** Open the secure testing browser/link, navigate to Canvas, and start your exam.
3. **Lab Rules During Testing:**
   - **Silence:** The lab is strictly silent at all times.
   - **Full Focus:** Do not look around at neighboring screens. Dividers and random seat assignments are in place for your privacy and fairness.
   - **Extra Scratch Paper:** If you fill both sides of your scratch paper, raise your hand. A proctor will exchange your filled sheet for a fresh one (you may only have one sheet at a time).
   - **Technical Difficulties:** If your computer freezes, loses network connectivity, or displays an error, immediately raise your hand. The proctor will assist you and note any technical interruption.

---

## 6. Submitting & Checking Out

When you finish your test or your allotted time expires:

1. **Submit Your Exam:** Ensure your exam is submitted properly on Canvas. Log out of the workstation.
2. **Gather Your Materials:** Pick up your pencil, your scratch paper, and your Duo passcode paper. Leave the workstation neat and push in your chair.
3. **Proceed to the Proctor Desk:**
   - Hand your used scratch paper, notes, and pencil to the proctor. All scratch paper is collected and shredded; **no paper may leave the testing center under any circumstances.**
4. **Proctor Check-Out & ID Retrieval:**
   - The proctor will scan your ID barcode or check you out on the console.
   - **Collect your Student ID card from the proctor.** Always make sure you have your physical card back before leaving the counter.
5. **Retrieve Belongings:** Collect your backpack and personal items from the cubby and exit the facility quietly.

---

## 7. Contingencies & Troubleshooting

### What if I miss my scheduled time?

- **15-Minute Grace Period:** If you arrive up to 15 minutes after your scheduled start time, the proctor can still check you in, but you will only receive the remainder of your original hour block (or whatever time remains before lab closing).
- **After 15 Minutes (Late / Missed):** If you are more than 15 minutes late, your reservation expires and your seat is released to prevent delays for other students. The proctor cannot check you in.
- **How to Reschedule:**
  1. Once marked as `MISSED`, go back to your Canvas course dashboard.
  2. Open the CBTF scheduling modal. You will see an option to **Reschedule Exam**.
  3. Pick a new date and time from the remaining open slots within your exam's testing window.
  4. _Note:_ Rescheduling is dependent entirely on remaining lab capacity. If all slots for the testing window are already filled, you will not be able to reschedule without direct instructor approval.

### What if I need to cancel or reschedule ahead of time?

If your schedule changes, you can reschedule or cancel your upcoming reservation at any time **before** your slot begins directly from the Canvas dashboard. Rescheduling early frees up the slot for a classmate and gives you more choices for an alternative time.

### Accommodations (SSD)

If you have registered testing accommodations (such as extended time or an ergonomic workstation) through Services for Students with Disabilities (SSD):

- Your approved time multiplier (e.g., 1.5x or 2.0x) is automatically applied to your booking duration.
- The scheduler will automatically assign you to certified accommodation workstations that meet your requirements.
- If you notice any discrepancy with your accommodation time when scheduling, contact your instructor before taking the exam.
