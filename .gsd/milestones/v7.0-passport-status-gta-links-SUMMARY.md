## 🎯 Executive Summary

Milestone v7.0 implements full support for the **PassPort Protocol v1.2** status query specification (`extension-api.v1.2.md`). External LTI tools connected via PassPort can now declare an `endpoints.status_handler` during dynamic registration or administrative configuration. The broker securely signs status queries via HMAC-SHA256, extracts student submission metadata (latest submission, submission for grading, scores, and instructor staff notices), caches the results on interview reservations, and presents actionable deep links and sanitized notices directly on GTA interview cards with screen-bound 15-minute staleness auto-refresh.
