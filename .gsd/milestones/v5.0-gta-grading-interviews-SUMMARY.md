# Milestone v5.0 Summary: Graduate TA Grading Interviews

> **Milestone Completed:** 2026-09-16
> **Status:** ✅ Complete
> **Coverage:** 100% Behavioral Coverage across database, server algorithms, student scheduling UI, and GTA dashboard

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Milestone v5.0 delivered a complete, course-integrated 1-on-1 grading interview scheduling system for assignments where students meet with Graduate Teaching Assistants (GTAs) to explain their work as part of the grading process. Adapting the scheduling and shift management architecture from the CBTF, this feature allows course staff to define recurring disconnected GTA shifts, calculate overlapping 10-minute appointment slots (5-minute interview + 5-minute prep), enable students to book interviews within a designated assignment window, prevent resubmission pass redemption until interviews are completed, and furnish GTAs with a dedicated check-in, checkout, and grading notes console.

---

## 🏗️ Deliverables Completed

- ✅ **Database Models & Migration**: `Course.interviewLocation`, `Assignment.hasInterviews`, `Assignment.interviewWindowStart/End`, `GtaShift`, and `GtaInterviewReservation`.
- ✅ **Role Check & Authorization**: Only users with course role `TA` or instructor can be scheduled.
- ✅ **Shift Parser & Batch Generator**: Recurring weekly disconnected shift builder for GTAs (instructors manage all, GTAs manage their own).
- ✅ **10-Minute Slot Availability Engine**: 5m interview + 5m prep with net capacity aggregated across overlapping GTAs on duty.
- ✅ **Half-Day Block Filtering**: Step 1 block view bounded by assignment interview window.
- ✅ **Student Booking API**: Automatic GTA assignment upon booking; confirmation includes GTA name and course meeting location.
- ✅ **Pass Redemption Gating**: Integrated with `redemptions.ts` to gate resubmission passes until interview completion.
- ✅ **GTA Console (`/interviews`)**: Expected arrivals, manual check-in, active interview panel with observation notes, checkout, and no-show marking.
- ✅ **Teacher Course & Assignment UI**: Configuration panels for interview requirement and meeting locations.
- ✅ **Student Scheduling Experience**: Dedicated 2-step booking dialog.
